Questions partners ask

Straight answers

What counts as a qualified lead?

A decision maker at a business with a real operational problem that AI can take on: lead follow-up, customer communication, document processing, or repetitive back-office work. Service businesses with a few to a few hundred employees are the sweet spot. You introduce them; we qualify them on the first call.

When and how do I get paid?

Commissions are calculated on net revenue as Silk Road receives it from the client and paid monthly. Net revenue means everything the client pays, setup and implementation fees included, less payment processing fees. You can see every line, calculated and paid, in your partner dashboard.

Do I have to sell anything?

Not as a referral partner. You make the introduction and Dallas runs the sale, the pilot, and the engagement. Contracted sales partners take a bigger share because they work the prospect from first contact through handoff, using decks and proposals Silk Road provides.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There is no fee, no quota, and no minimum. We sign a short partner agreement, you get a partner code and a referral link, and you earn when accounts you introduce become paying clients.

Who gets credit if two people introduce the same business?

The partner who registered the account first. Booking through your referral link registers it to you automatically; a lead you send by email is logged against your code the day it arrives. A registered account counts as yours if it signs and makes its first payment within 180 days of registration.

What do you actually build for clients?

Done-for-you AI systems: lead follow-up that answers in seconds, customer communication in the client's voice, document processing into the software they already run, and dashboards they can run the company on. Every engagement starts with a 30-day pilot measured against numbers the client already tracks. See what we do.

Book a 30-minute intro call. We'll set you up as a partner the same day, and your first referral can go out that afternoon.
