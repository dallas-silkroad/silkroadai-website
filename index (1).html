Trust & compliance

Built to be trusted with your data

Operators care about the details. So do we.

HIPAA-aware deployments with a BAA

Where a workflow touches protected health information, we sign a Business Associate Agreement before anything is built and limit the health data involved to what the workflow needs.

Your data stays yours

Client data lives in the client's own accounts, is processed only as instructed, and is never used to train general-purpose AI models.

Meet the founder

Dallas Wymes

Founder, Silk Road AI Solutions · Listed in Marquis Who's Who

Dallas Wymes spent 35 years building and running the operating systems behind global supply chains. He started at Air Express International in 1991, became an owner and executive at Kamino International Logistics, led national accounts at ABX Logistics and startup operations at Crane Worldwide, and spent 16 years as a vice president at Mainfreight, scaling its Americas warehousing division. Every one of those roles came down to the same job: find the bottleneck, fix the process, and make the numbers move.

Silk Road AI Solutions is the direct line from that work to AI. Dallas applies the same operator's discipline to the front end of a business, where leads, customer communication, documents and follow-up either get handled or get lost. We don't hand you software and wish you luck. We design, build, and run the AI for you, and we measure it the way an operator would.
